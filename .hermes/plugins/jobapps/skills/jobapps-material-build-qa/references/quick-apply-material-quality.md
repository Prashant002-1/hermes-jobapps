# Quick-apply material quality

`Quick apply`, `low priority`, or `use a bucket resume` means reuse a proven structure and limit tailoring time. It does not lower the truth, readability, or artifact-quality bar.

1. Start from the closest confirmed template.
2. Make only targeted role shifts.
3. Keep one-page, contamination, filename, render, and clean-folder checks.
4. If the page is visibly underfilled, restore relevant confirmed evidence before adding filler.
5. Preserve JD skills only when they are supported by profile facts or proof points.
6. Stop after one consolidated repair pass unless rendering exposes a new defect.

Speed should come from reuse and bounded iteration, not from shipping a sparse or unverified document.
