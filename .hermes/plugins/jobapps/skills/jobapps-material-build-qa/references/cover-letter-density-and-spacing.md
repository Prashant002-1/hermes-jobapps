# Cover-letter density and spacing QA

Use this when a cover letter is technically one page but feels cramped or visually unbalanced.

## Repair order

1. Preserve approved narrative and confirmed claims.
2. Keep a professional letter structure: company/role context, salutation, readable paragraphs, and closing.
3. Adjust margins, paragraph leading, paragraph spacing, and small vertical gaps before cutting content.
4. If only the signature spills to page two, tighten the closing gap first.
5. Recompile after every source change.
6. Confirm one page, clean extracted text, no overflow/cutoff, and acceptable rendered whitespace.

Do not rewrite the letter from scratch to solve a layout-only request. Make the smallest readable change and report the final artifact briefly.
